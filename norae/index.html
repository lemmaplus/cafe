Current sample · IU — Someday
Each Hangul syllable is a tiny construction: an initial consonant, a vowel, and sometimes 받침 (batchim) — a consonant in the final, bottom slot of the syllable. We begin with IU’s Someday. The song is not explained here; it is sorted, cracked open, and looked at.
