How many tables could there be?

A lot — until you forget the berry names. “Up to relabeling” is the same as “up to homeomorphism” here.

No more legal bundles? You’ve reached the T₀ skeleton. Bundle together berries that every open set treats the same. Once no two different fruits can still be bundled, every pair can be distinguished — that quotient is T₀.
So every topology on n labeled berries can be built in two steps: partition the berries into k indistinguishable bundles, then choose a labeled T₀ topology on those k bundles. That is what t(n) = Σ S(n,k)·p(k) counts: S(n,k) choices of bundles, followed by p(k) choices of T₀ skeleton.
Current census note (2026): exact totals for labeled topologies are known through 18 points; the unlabeled / homeomorphism-class sequence is tabulated through 16 points. There is still no general closed formula.
